Dr. I. William Grossman MLT to MLS Scholarship

This scholarship is to help students who have completed their associates degree in Medical Laboratory Technology and 1 year in the DH clinical laboratory and wish to continue their education in Medical Laboratory Science to achieve their bachelor’s degree. This educational pursuit will allow individuals to perform competencies and apply for lead and supervisory positions. Upon completion these students will receive their bachelor’s degree and will be eligible to sit for the ASCP MLS exam and apply for a Medical Laboratory Technologist position in the clinical laboratory.

The Dr. I. William Grossman MLT to MLS Scholarship Fund provides scholarship assistance to students currently employed by Dartmouth Health to further their education from MLT to MLS.

  • Be employed by Dartmouth Health or affiliate
  • Complete an associate degree program in Medical Laboratory Technology

Scholarship application is due 14 days prior to the start of each semester.

You may receive a scholarship up to $1500 per academic semester/term, for a cumulative total of $6000, based on any gap in tuition coverage you have after utilizing your employer’s tuition reimbursement, other scholarship sources or financial aid.
